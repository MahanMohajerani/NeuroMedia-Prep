\# Changelog



\## v1.0.0



Initial stable release.



\### Features



\- Long video splitting into timed sessions

\- Fast and Exact processing modes

\- Natural Cut Points workspace

\- Subtitle synchronization and burn-in

\- Persian, Arabic, and Unicode subtitle support

\- Device-oriented output profiles

\- FFmpeg and FFprobe first-run checks

\- Local-only media processing

\- Windows 10 and Windows 11 support



\### Validation



\- AVI output tested on neurofeedback hardware

\- Persian subtitle rendering tested
